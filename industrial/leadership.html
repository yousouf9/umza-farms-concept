Executive Leadership

The founders and operators.

Alhaji Mohammed Abubakar Maifata

Founder & Chairman, Umza Group

Founder of Umza Group, a diversified business group spanning agriculture, agro-processing, logistics, trading, and aviation. As Chairman of Umza International Farms Limited, he has directed the company's transformation into a diversified food-processing platform with established interests in rice milling and edible oil production.

His focus on local sourcing, farmer partnerships, and modern processing technology has shaped Umza's operating model. He is also recognized for supporting education, healthcare, and youth-empowerment initiatives across Northern Nigeria.

Malam Adamu Ali Wudil

Farm Manager

Former Director of State Projects and Coordinator of the Third National Fadama Development Programme, Kano State Government. Holds an M.Ds. in Development Studies from Bayero University, Kano.

Professional exposure to agricultural development practices in the Philippines, Egypt, Malawi, Ethiopia, and Israel informs his approach to Umza's field operations.
