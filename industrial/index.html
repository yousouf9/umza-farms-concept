Company Record

Operating history, on the record.

Six milestones from incorporation to our latest infrastructure investment.

2004
01 / INCORPORATION

Umza International Farms Limited is incorporated, establishing the foundation for long-term participation in Nigeria's agro-processing sector.

2009
02 / MILLING BEGINS

Commercial rice processing begins in Kano at an initial annual capacity of 70,000 metric tonnes.

2015–20
03 / SOURCING SCALE

Farmer and outgrower network expands; Umza participates as an off-taker under the CBN Anchor Borrowers' Programme in Kebbi State.

2020–22
04 / CAPACITY

Processing infrastructure investment lifts annual capacity to ~200,000 MT; land allocated in Jigawa (150 ha) and Bauchi (7,000 ha) States.

2023–24
05 / DIVERSIFICATION

Umza Oil Mills launches — Rice Bran Oil and Soya Bean Oil join the product portfolio.

2026
06 / ENERGY

Hybrid 1MWp solar + 2.15MWh battery storage plant commissioned at the Kano facility.
