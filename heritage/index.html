Our Business

Two businesses, one integrated platform.

Umza Rice Mill and Umza Oil Mills operate as complementary businesses — rice bran generated during milling becomes the raw material for our Rice Bran Oil, connecting sourcing, processing, and product in one supply chain.

Umza Rice Mill

~200,000 MT/year processing capacity

Founded on rice, our milling operation processes locally sourced paddy into four consumer brands — Umza Gold, Umza Classic, 3 Tomato Rice, and Kings Choice — packaged without added preservatives for households, retailers, and institutions.

Umza Oil Mills

Rice Bran & Soya Bean Oil

Launched in 2023–2024, our edible oil business produces Rice Bran Oil from bran generated during our own milling process, alongside Soya Bean Oil — a direct link between our two core businesses.

Our History

Two decades of building, one harvest at a time.

From a single rice mill in Kano to an integrated agribusiness with a growing edible-oil business and a renewable-energy-powered plant.

2004

Umza International Farms Limited is incorporated, laying the foundation for long-term participation in Nigeria's agro-processing sector.

2009

Commercial rice processing begins in Kano with an initial annual capacity of 70,000 metric tonnes — rice becomes the cornerstone of the business.

2015 – 2020

The farmer and outgrower network expands; Umza participates as an off-taker under the CBN Anchor Borrowers' Programme in Kebbi State.

2020 – 2022

Continued investment in processing infrastructure lifts annual capacity to ~200,000 metric tonnes; land allocations follow in Jigawa (150 ha) and Bauchi (7,000 ha) States.

2023 – 2024

Diversification into edible oil production with the launch of Umza Oil Mills — Rice Bran Oil and Soya Bean Oil join the portfolio.

2026

Umza commissions a hybrid 1MWp solar and 2.15MWh battery storage plant at its Kano facility — a step toward cleaner, more reliable industrial energy.

Outgrower Programme

A structured platform connecting local farmers to industrial-scale processing.

During the 2023/2024 season, we organized 54 farmer groups across 1,433 hectares in Auyo, Jigawa State, providing seeds, fertilizer, and agrochemicals under an input-financing arrangement, repayable in kind after harvest.
