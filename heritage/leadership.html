Executive Leadership

The founders and operators.

Alhaji Mohammed Abubakar Maifata

Founder & Chairman, Umza Group

A Nigerian entrepreneur and Founder of Umza Group, a diversified business group with interests in agriculture, agro-processing, logistics, trading and aviation. As Founder and Chairman of Umza International Farms Limited, he has shaped the company's transformation from an agricultural enterprise into a diversified food-processing company with established interests in rice milling and edible oil production.

His commitment to Nigeria's agricultural sector is reflected in Umza's focus on local sourcing, farmer partnerships, modern processing technology, and the efficient utilization of agricultural resources. He is recognized for a practical management approach, strategic perspective, and emphasis on integrity and operational excellence. Beyond his commercial interests, he supports initiatives in education, healthcare, and youth empowerment, with a particular focus on communities in Northern Nigeria.

Malam Adamu Ali Wudil

Farm Manager

Born in 1958, Malam Wudil is an experienced agricultural professional with a strong background in public-sector project coordination and agricultural development. He served as Director of State Projects and Coordinator of the Third National Fadama Development Programme under the Kano State Government.

His professional experience includes agricultural training programmes, workshops, and seminars in Nigeria and abroad, with exposure to development practices in the Philippines, Egypt, Malawi, Ethiopia, and Israel. He holds a Master's Degree in Development Studies (M.Ds.) from Bayero University, Kano.
