Executive Leadership

The founders and operators.

Alhaji Mohammed Abubakar Maifata

Founder & Chairman

Founder of Umza Group, leading its growth from a rice-focused business into a diversified food-processing company with interests in agriculture, logistics, trading, and aviation.

His focus on local sourcing and farmer partnerships shapes everything Umza makes — and he's an active supporter of education, healthcare, and youth-empowerment causes across Northern Nigeria.

Malam Adamu Ali Wudil

Farm Manager

Decades of experience in agricultural development — from coordinating Kano State's Fadama programme to hands-on farm management today. Holds a Master's in Development Studies from Bayero University, Kano.

His international training spans the Philippines, Egypt, Malawi, Ethiopia, and Israel.
