Our Story

Two decades in the making.

Swipe through the milestones that got us here.

2004

Umza International Farms Limited is incorporated in Kano.

2009

Commercial rice milling begins — 70,000 MT/year initial capacity.

2015–20

Outgrower network expands; joins CBN's Anchor Borrowers' Programme.

2020–22

Capacity grows to ~200,000 MT/year; new land allocated in Jigawa & Bauchi.

2023–24

Umza Oil Mills launches — Rice Bran & Soya Bean Oil join the family.

2026

Hybrid solar + battery plant commissioned at our Kano facility.
